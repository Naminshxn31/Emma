## head-turnaround-v4.png

Reference: `client/rive/emma/assets/head.png`.

The generated atlas has its side directions reversed from the prompt. The generator deliberately maps cell 0 to looking right and cell 2 to looking left, after visual inspection.

Prompt:

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: a single transparent sprite atlas for an existing Rive robot. Image 1 is the exact Emma head identity reference. Produce a horizontal THREE-CELL TURNAROUND of this same detached polished-chrome and pearl-white robot head, with NO EYES, NO eyebrows, NO nose, NO neck, NO body, NO writing. Keep the exact unusual smile-shaped chrome lower face/bezel of image 1, its pearlescent domed white upper face, thick glossy chrome rim, curved white lower jaw. Do not redesign it.
LEFT CELL: head actually rotated 24 degrees toward the viewer's LEFT, clearly showing a volumetric rounded chrome shell on the RIGHT rear side.
CENTER CELL: exact straight FRONT view.
RIGHT CELL: head actually rotated 24 degrees toward the viewer's RIGHT, showing the rounded chrome rear shell on the LEFT.
These are three camera views of one solid rounded 3D head, NOT flat oval faces stretched or skewed. It has physical depth comparable to half its width. Keep camera eye-level, no roll, no pitch. All three heads same height, same chin baseline, same spherical volume and studio lighting. White bright studio reflection above-left, deep black chrome reflection bands, narrow brilliant silver edge highlights. Keep the materials and shape very close to the reference, not a blue screen, not a generic sphere.
Composition: wide canvas, three equal square cells side by side, completely separate with generous transparent gutters. Every head is centered in its cell, occupies 84% of the cell height. All fully visible with 8% margin. True transparent alpha behind and between heads, no background or ground shadow, no checkerboard, no text or labels. Only these three blank-faced chrome heads.

## palm-open-v4.png

Reference: `client/rive/emma/assets/arm-right.png`.

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one transparent 2D animation sprite. Image 1 is the Emma robot hand material reference. Create ONE isolated Emma RIGHT glove hand showing the PALM clearly toward the viewer for a friendly hello wave. Glossy pearly white plump rounded toy fingers, short polished chrome wrist cuff with black inner band, same material as reference. Four rounded fingers extending upward with a gentle natural fan, thumb on the viewer's LEFT spreading outward, welcoming open palm visibly cupped and facing camera; not a side view, not the back of the hand, not curled fingers pointing down. Wrist cuff at bottom center, palm above it, fingers pointing straight up. The cuff is one SHORT oval chrome cuff, no long forearm, no arm, no body, no extra parts. Bright soft studio lighting above-left, natural pearl gradients and chrome black-white reflections, clean sculpted anatomy, no glove stitching or fingernails. Entire hand and cuff fully contained on square canvas, centered with 15% transparent margin. Real alpha transparent background, no shadows on a backdrop, no text, no checkerboard.
